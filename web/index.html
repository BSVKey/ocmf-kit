Check a charging receipt

German charging stations sign each meter reading (the OCMF format). Paste the signed data and the meter's public key; this page checks that the readings are genuine and complete, and shows the energy you were billed for. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1. Paste the signed readings

From your charging receipt, app or operator. Each record starts with OCMF|. OCPP messages with SignedData or signedMeterValue also work.

Take it from the label on the charger or the operator's key list. Do not trust a key that came with the data: anyone who changed the data could change that key too.
